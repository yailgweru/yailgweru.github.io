Young AI Leaders Gweru Hub — Youth AI, Tech & Social Innovation in Gweru, Zimbabwe & Southern Africa

Youth-led AI hub in Gweru, Zimbabwe — young leaders building ethical AI, digital skills and social impact for communities across Zimbabwe and Southern Africa.

Young AI Leaders Gweru Hub is a youth-led AI and technology community in Gweru, Midlands Province, Zimbabwe, and a hub of the ITU AI for Good Young AI Leaders Community. We connect young innovators across Zimbabwe and Southern Africa through ethical artificial intelligence, community technology, social innovation and hands-on AI literacy — rooted in local languages, local context and youth leadership.

About — Who we are

Our mission

Empowering Gweru’s youth to build ethical, responsible and African-rooted AI that is localised for our languages, our context and Africa’s unique challenges.

The hub

A tight-knit African youth tech community of young AI leaders in Gweru, Midlands Province — collaborating on real projects with community impact across Zimbabwe and Southern Africa.

AI for Good network

We are the Gweru Hub of the global Young AI Leaders Community, an ITU AI for Good initiative connecting African youth tech hubs with innovators worldwide.

Projects — What we build

AI Literacy for All

Online and in-person sessions that equip people with practical knowledge of AI tools and best practices.

Online and in-person sessions that equip people with practical knowledge of AI tools and best practices.

Looking at the surrounding community, we realised that most people only used a handful of AI tools. We set out to introduce them to a wider range of tools and show what each one can do.

Using a few of those tools, we showed how AI can do far more than chat. The sessions covered tools that assist with research, study and media generation, along with techniques to get the best output from them.

We closed each session by highlighting the risks of AI and how to guard against them.
